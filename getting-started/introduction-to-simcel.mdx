Welcome to **SIMCEL** — your strategic command center for modern supply chain planning. Designed for agility and precision, SIMCEL helps organizations streamline operations, elevate decision-making, and accelerate toward business goals with confidence.

## Why SIMCEL exists

Today's supply chains are more complex than ever — fragmented data, siloed planning, volatile demand, and increasing sustainability expectations. SIMCEL unifies these moving parts into a single platform, empowering teams to plan, simulate, and optimize collaboratively.

At its core, SIMCEL delivers:

- End-to-end visibility across supply and demand.
- Seamless integration of planning layers (Demand, Supply, Finance).
- Fast, scenario-based decision-making that's grounded in reality.

## Key features at a glance

SIMCEL offers a powerful suite of features to meet the diverse needs of supply chain teams.

### Demand planning

Forecast demand with high accuracy using **advanced machine learning**. Adjust forecasts using trade events, marketing inputs, or market signals — and proactively shape future demand, not just react to it.

### Stock-in-trade management

Ensure healthy inventories at the distributor level. Track **Sales In, inventory days, and stock health** to avoid overstock or missed sales.

### Supply planning

Turn demand signals into action. Build **constrained supply plans**, simulate production and inventory flows, and optimize availability across the network.

### Business Explorer (scenario planning)

Model and compare scenarios: what if demand drops 20%? What if a plant shuts down? Business Explorer lets you test these possibilities before they happen, with **granular KPI impact analysis**.

### Financial modeling

Bridge the gap between operations and finance. Evaluate performance across **Revenue, Gross Margins, Trade Spend, SG&A, and Supply Chain Costs** — aligning supply plans with P&L targets.

### Reporting and analytics

Access intuitive dashboards and customizable reports. Get insights into your most critical KPIs and monitor progress toward business goals.

### CO2 computation

Track the environmental impact of your supply chain. SIMCEL computes **CO2 emissions** by activity and flow, helping you measure, report, and reduce your carbon footprint.
