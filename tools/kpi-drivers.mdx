To access the Business Drivers widget, click **KPI Drivers** under the Tools section on the left-side panel.

<Frame caption="Opening KPI Drivers from the Tools section of the left-side panel">
  ![The SIMCEL left-side navigation panel with the Tools section expanded and KPI Drivers highlighted](/images/tools/kpi-drivers-1.png)
</Frame>

This widget lets you explore the best and worst-performing segments according to the KPIs you select.

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define parameters">
    - **Dimension 1 (x-axis)**: Select the first category to segment data (for example, product ID or category).
    - **Dimension 2 (x-axis)**: Select the second category for further segmentation.
    - **Display**: Choose the top 10 or bottom 10 values for KPI 1 or KPI 2.
    - **KPI 1 (bar, left y-axis)**: Choose the first key performance indicator.
    - **KPI 2 (line, right y-axis)**: Choose the second key performance indicator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze results">
    The chart updates automatically to display the relevant data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The KPI Drivers chart, with KPI 1 as bars and KPI 2 as a line">
  ![KPI Drivers chart showing KPI 1 as bars against the left y-axis and KPI 2 as a line against the right y-axis, with the selected dimensions on the x-axis and the parameter selectors above the chart](/images/tools/kpi-drivers-2.png)
</Frame>

## Example

**Scenario**: Identify the products with the highest demand and evaluate their service levels country wide.

- **Dimension 1**: Product ID
- **Dimension 2**: Country
- **Display**: KPI 1 - Top 10
- **KPI 1**: Unconstrained Gross Sales In Value
- **KPI 2**: Service Level
- **Action**: Investigate low service levels, alert headquarters to increase supply, and develop a plan to improve service levels.

## KPI driver chain

The P&L waterfall shows how KPIs cascade from revenue to profit:

```text
Gross FIE Sales
  - FIE Trade Expenses
  = Net FIE Sales
    - Distributor COGS
    = Gross Profit
      - Transport
      - Storage & Handling
      = Supply Chain Cost
        - Promotion
        - Medical Affairs
        = DPM
          - Administration
          = EBIT
```

Use the chain to trace a change in an outcome KPI such as EBIT back to the input KPI driving it, to see which downstream KPIs a driver affects, and to decide which KPIs to prioritize in planning.
