---
title: "KPI Tracker Table"
description: "Summarize a selected KPI for a scenario over a period of time to spot trends and compare performance."
---

The **KPI Tracker Table** lets you select a KPI to summarize for the selected scenario over a period of time. Use it to:

- Track specific KPIs over time.
- Compare performance across different scenarios.
- Make informed decisions based on KPI trends.
