---
title: "Explore Actual"
description: "Visualize historical demand and analyze performance by scenario and segment."
---

The **Explore Actual** feature in SIMCEL lets you dive deep into your historical demand performance. By analyzing past data, you can spot trends, assess planning accuracy, and build a stronger foundation for future forecasts.

## Navigation

To get started:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Demand Planning module">
    Click the **Demand** option on the left-hand sidebar, under the **IBP** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Actual dataset">
    From the available datasets, select **Actual** to begin exploring your historical sales data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The Demand option in the left sidebar, under the IBP section">
  ![SIMCEL left sidebar with the Demand option highlighted under the IBP section, opening the Demand Planning module](/images/demand/explore-actual-1.png)
</Frame>

By selecting the **Actual** dataset, you can explore your historical data.

<Frame caption="The Demand Planning page with the Actual dataset selected">
  ![Demand Planning view showing the Actual dataset selected, displaying historical sales data across the planning horizon](/images/demand/explore-actual-2.png)
</Frame>

## Explore the demand chart

In the **Demand Chart** widget:

- **Grey lines** represent your actual historical performance.
- **Purple lines** show previously committed plans.

This line chart lets you visualize demand trends over time, and totals are shown for each selected period on the left-hand side. You can toggle specific lines on and off by clicking their labels in the chart legend.

<Frame caption="Actual performance shown in grey in the Demand Chart">
  ![Demand Chart widget with grey lines representing actual historical demand performance over time](/images/demand/explore-actual-3.png)
</Frame>

**Committed plans** in purple show the previous plans that were committed for execution.

<Frame caption="Committed plans shown in purple in the Demand Chart">
  ![Demand Chart widget with purple lines representing previously committed plans alongside actual demand](/images/demand/explore-actual-4.png)
</Frame>

The **Demand Chart** widget presents a line chart that visualizes demand over time and displays the total for the selected period on the left-hand side for each scenario. To hide or show specific lines, click their corresponding entries in the legend.

<Frame caption="Scenario totals for the selected period shown beside the Demand Chart">
  ![Demand Chart widget showing per-scenario totals for the selected period on the left-hand side, with a clickable legend to toggle lines](/images/demand/explore-actual-5.png)
</Frame>

## Explore the demand table

Beneath the chart, you'll find the **Demand Table**. This lets you analyze demand by segment per scenario, offering a granular view of how different customer or product groups behave over time.

<Frame caption="The Demand Table beneath the Demand Chart">
  ![Demand Table listing demand by segment for each scenario, with expandable rows](/images/demand/explore-actual-6.png)
</Frame>

To see the detailed demand per segment for each dataset, use the **&gt;** button to expand segment details and view deeper breakdowns.

<Frame caption="Expanded segment details in the Demand Table">
  ![Demand Table with a segment row expanded using the chevron button, revealing a deeper breakdown of demand per segment](/images/demand/explore-actual-7.png)
</Frame>
