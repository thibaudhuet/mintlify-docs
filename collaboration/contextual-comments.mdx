## Definition

Contextual comments let you collaborate directly inside object drawers. Instead of managing feedback in separate tools, comments are embedded in the planning view, keeping discussions tied to the right context. All communication, decisions, and attachments stay visible and connected to the relevant object.

## Adding a comment

Open the **Comments** tab inside any object's drawer to post a comment. Comments appear directly under the object they relate to, so your input is always visible in context.

Mention teammates using `@username` with autocomplete to notify them directly.

<Frame caption="Posting a comment in an object drawer and mentioning a teammate">
  ![Comments tab in an object drawer with a comment being typed and the @mention autocomplete suggesting a teammate](/images/collaboration/contextual-comments-1.gif)
</Frame>

When posted, the comment is stored in the thread, complete with the author's name, timestamp, mentions, and attachments.

## Grouping and ordering

All comments are **grouped by day**, with the newest groups always displayed at the top of the thread. Within a group, comments appear in chronological order, preserving the natural flow of discussion. This lets you quickly catch up on the most recent conversations without losing track of earlier exchanges.

<Frame caption="Comments grouped by day, newest first">

## Managing and reacting to comments

You can interact with comments in several ways to keep conversations clear and engaging.

**Edit** or **Delete** your own comments to correct mistakes or remove outdated information.

<Frame caption="Edit and delete controls on your own comment">
  ![A posted comment showing the edit and delete controls available to its author](/images/collaboration/contextual-comments-3.png)
</Frame>

**Like** or **Dislike** any comment to quickly signal agreement, acknowledgment, or disagreement without starting a new thread.

<Frame caption="Like and dislike reactions on a comment">
  ![A comment with like and dislike reaction buttons for signalling agreement or disagreement](/images/collaboration/contextual-comments-4.png)
</Frame>

This combination keeps comments accurate while also allowing lightweight feedback from the team.
