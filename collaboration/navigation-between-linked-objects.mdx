---
title: "Navigation between linked objects"
description: "Move between linked Plans, Scenarios, and Events in drawers without losing context."
---

From one drawer, you can jump to related objects, for example from a Plan to its Scenarios, or from a Scenario to its Events. A **back** option lets you return to the previous drawer, so you never lose context. SIMCEL also preserves your **scroll position** when you go back, so you can pick up exactly where you left off.

<Frame caption="Jumping from a Plan drawer through to a linked Scenario and back">
  ![Object drawer navigation showing a jump from a Plan to one of its linked Scenarios and a return to the previous drawer with the scroll position preserved](/images/collaboration/navigation-between-linked-objects-1.gif)
</Frame>

For example:

- Start with a **Plan** to see its global setup and connected scenarios.
- Drill down into a **Scenario** to understand assumptions, relationships, and linked events.
- Select an **Event** within the **Scenario** drawer to review the adjustments or changes driving demand within that scenario.

This structured, layered presentation helps you move from high-level planning down to specific events while always keeping the right context in view.
