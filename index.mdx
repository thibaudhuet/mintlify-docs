SIMCEL is a SaaS Integrated Business Planning (IBP) platform that brings together your Demand, Supply, Finance, and Carbon planning into one unified workspace. Plan smarter, simulate scenarios, and make data-driven decisions across your entire supply chain.

## Why SIMCEL exists

Today's supply chains are more complex than ever — fragmented data, siloed planning, volatile demand, and increasing sustainability expectations. SIMCEL unifies these moving parts into a single platform, empowering teams to plan, simulate, and optimize collaboratively.

At its core, SIMCEL delivers:

- End-to-end visibility across supply and demand.
- Seamless integration of planning layers (Demand, Supply, Finance).
- Fast, scenario-based decision-making that's grounded in reality.

## Key capabilities

- **Scenario planning** — create and compare multiple planning scenarios side by side
- **Event modeling** — simulate the impact of promotions, pricing changes, trade terms, and new demand streams
- **50+ KPIs** — automatically calculated metrics covering demand, supply, finance, and emissions
- **Supply simulation** — run supply chain simulations and see constrained results
- **Collaborative planning** — work with your team using comments, tasks, and shared scenarios
- **Multi-currency support** — plan across regions with automatic currency conversion
