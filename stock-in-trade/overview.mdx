## Term definitions

- **SIT (Stock In Trade)** - The stock level (in days) for the specified distributor and product at the given period end.
- **Closing Stock** - The distributor's inventory records at the period's end (either in unit or value).
- **Sales-Out** - Sales from the distributors to their customers (pharmacies, hospitals, and so on). Also referred to as secondary sales (either in unit or value).
- **Sales In** - Sales to the distributors. Also referred to as primary sales (either in unit or value).
- **Distributor GSV** - The price at which the company sells the product to its distributors.
- **Customer GSV** - The price at which the distributor sells the product to its customers.

## SIT modes: unconstrained vs constrained

SIMCEL operates in two distinct SIT planning modes, each reflecting a different stage of the scenario lifecycle and the nature of data used in the calculations.

<Frame caption="The SIT mode indicator distinguishing unconstrained and constrained planning">
  ![SIT page showing the mode indicator for unconstrained versus constrained planning](/images/stock-in-trade/overview-8.png)
</Frame>

### 1. Unconstrained mode

This is the default mode when a scenario is created but **before simulation is triggered**. All KPIs on the SIT page are calculated based on:

- **Unconstrained Secondary Sales** - Planned downstream sales from distributor to market, prior to any restriction from stock availability.
- **Unconstrained Primary Sales** - Planned inbound supply from upstream (for example, corporate to distributor), without taking supply constraints into account.
- **Gross Value** basis for financials - discounts, rebates, and other trade terms are not applied yet.

In **Unconstrained Mode**, all metrics are editable except Secondary Sales, which is populated from the demand plan.

### 2. Constrained mode

This mode becomes available **after a simulation is run**. The simulation aligns product availability (based on inventory and supply plan) with demand, resulting in constrained flows. KPIs are recalculated based on:

- **Constrained Primary Sales** - Actual feasible supply to distributor based on inventory and replenishment limits.
- **Constrained Secondary Sales** - Expected downstream sales based on Sales In and available inventory at the distributor.
- Still computed on a **Gross Value** basis in the SIT module (even if trade terms are simulated elsewhere).

In **Constrained Mode**, none of the metrics are editable.

In the SIT table, numbers highlighted with a subtle grey background can be edited. You can adjust the following:

- Stock Days (days)
- Stock Value (unit or value)

### Editing SIT target / closing stock

When you create a scenario, SIMCEL uses the standard SIT targets (defined per product-distributor) and computes the other metrics accordingly.

When you edit the SIT target or Closing Stock for a specific segment, the system carries out these steps:

1. Calculate the related Closing Stock / SIT target, and Sales In at that level using the new SIT value.
2. Update the Closing Stock, Sales In, and SIT at that level for the following months.
3. Re-calculate the Closing Stock values for the child levels based on the percentage increase or decrease of the direct parent.
4. Re-calculate the impact on Sales In and SIT for the child level, including the following months.
5. Adjust the Closing Stock and Sales In values for the parent levels to ensure they equal the total of their direct children.
6. Re-calculate the SIT for the parent level.

<Note>
  Refer to the **Calculation Logic** section for the specific formulas.
</Note>

### Editing Sales In

When you edit the Sales In for a specific segment, the system carries out these steps:

1. Calculate the related Closing Stock and SIT at that level using the new Sales In value.
2. Update the Closing Stock, Sales In, and SIT at that level for the following months.
3. Re-allocate the Sales In values for the child levels based on the percentage increase or decrease of the direct parent.
4. Re-calculate the impact on Closing Stock and SIT for the child level.
5. Adjust the Sales In and Closing Stock values for the parent levels to ensure they equal the total of the child levels.
6. Re-calculate the SIT for the parent level.

<Note>
  Refer to the **Calculation Logic** section for the specific formulas.
</Note>

### Quick SIT metrics setting

In the SIT table, you can configure SIT metrics (Stock Days, Stock Value, Primary Sales) across a continuous range of months by entering a single value in a selected month and applying it forward through the remaining plan timeline.

To do a quick SIT metrics setting for a scenario:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the starting month and metric">
    Select the starting month and the metric you want to edit quickly.

    <Frame caption="Selecting the starting month and metric for a quick setting">
      ![SIT table with a starting month column and a metric row selected for quick editing](/images/stock-in-trade/overview-10.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new value">
    Click the number within that month and metric. An edit box appears for you to make changes.

    <Frame caption="Entering a new value in the edit box">
      ![SIT table cell opened into an edit box with a new value being typed](/images/stock-in-trade/overview-11.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the value forward">
    After you enter the new number, look to the left of the edit box to find a checkmark with a right arrow. Click it.

    <Frame caption="The checkmark with a right arrow that applies the value forward">
      ![Edit box in the SIT table showing the checkmark and right-arrow control to the left](/images/stock-in-trade/overview-12.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in the dialogue">
    A dialogue appears, showing:

    1. The SIT metric being quickly set (Stock Days, Stock Value, or Primary Sales). For example, **Stock Days**.
    2. The edited month. For example, **2026-04-01**.
    3. The ending month of the plan. For example, **2027-03-31**.

    <Frame caption="The quick setting confirmation dialogue">
      ![Dialogue showing the SIT metric, the edited month, the plan end month, and an Apply button](/images/stock-in-trade/overview-13.png)
    </Frame>

    Click **Apply** to confirm your choice and let the system recalculate everything to align with your new settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>
